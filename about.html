About Finance Tools

Free, private financial calculators that run entirely in your browser.

What is Finance Tools?

Finance Tools is a collection of free financial calculators covering compound interest, mortgage payments, retirement planning, savings goals, investment returns, dividend yield, inflation, and more.

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser — the numbers you type are never sent to a server, and no account is required. The site is free to use and is funded by clearly labelled advertising; see our Privacy Policy for how that works.
