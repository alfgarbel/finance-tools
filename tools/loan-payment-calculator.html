What Is a Loan Payment?

A loan payment is the fixed amount you pay each month to repay borrowed money plus the cost of borrowing (interest). With a standard fixed-rate loan, every payment is the same size for the entire term.

Each payment is split into two parts:

  • Principal — the portion that reduces the outstanding balance of the loan.
  • Interest — the fee the lender charges for use of their money, calculated on the remaining balance.

Early in the loan most of your payment covers interest. Over time the balance decreases, so the interest portion shrinks and more of each payment goes toward principal. This gradual payoff process is called amortization.

Loan Payment Formula

The standard formula for a fixed-rate loan payment is:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n − 1 ] M = monthly payment, P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12), n = total number of payments (years × 12)

When the interest rate is 0%, the formula simplifies to: M = P / n (the loan amount divided evenly across all payments).

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of loans does this calculator work for?

This calculator works for any fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan, including personal loans, auto loans, and student loans. It assumes equal monthly payments over the entire term. It does not account for variable rates, interest-only periods, or balloon payments.

How does the loan term affect total interest?

A longer loan term lowers your monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay because the balance accrues interest for more months. A shorter term raises monthly payments but saves a significant amount in interest over the life of the loan. Choose the shortest term you can comfortably afford to minimize total cost.

Does this calculator include fees or insurance?

No. This calculator computes principal and interest only. Actual loan costs may include origination fees, closing costs, or required insurance. Check with your lender for a full cost breakdown before committing to a loan.
